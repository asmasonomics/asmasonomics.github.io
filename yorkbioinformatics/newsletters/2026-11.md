  - title: BioFAIR Pathfinder projects 2026
    image: /yorkbioinformatics/assets/logos/BioFAIR.png
    description: |
      Pathfinder Projects offer researchers and research technical professionals the opportunity to tackle real-world barriers to making life sciences data FAIR and AI-ready. We invite innovative proposals that diagnose challenges and prototype practical, data-driven solutions, grounded in a scientific question. Pathfinder Projects will act as exemplars, driving requirements for the BioFAIR initiative and developing proofs-of-concept that can benefit from, and be incorporated into, the national infrastructure (in particular, BioFAIR’s upcoming Data Commons in 2027).<br/>
      We will fund 10–12 projects across the scientific domains. Successful projects will receive up to £100,000 funding for 12 months to test new approaches, drive culture change, and accelerate the adoption of FAIR practices. Don’t miss this chance to help shape the future of UK life sciences research infrastructure.<br/>
      Deadline: 15 December 2026. <a href="https://biofair.uk/pathfinder-projects-2026/">Info and application form here</a>.

      - title: Introduction to multi-omics data integration and visualisation
        link: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/training/events/introduction-multi-omics-data-integration-and-visualisation-2027/
        image: /yorkbioinformatics/assets/logos/EMBL-EBI.jpg
        description: |
          This introductory course will highlight the challenges that researchers face in integrating multi-omics datasets. The course will focus on biological examples in a practice oriented way,  using public data resources and open access tools, with an emphasis on visualisation. This course will not include systems biology modelling or machine learning. A major element of the course is a group project, where you will work together with 2-4 other participants on a challenge set by trainers from EMBL-EBI data resources and research teams. This allows you to explore the bioinformatics tools and resources introduced in the course and to apply these to a well defined research problem, providing hands-on experience relevant to your own research. The group work will culminate in a presentation session involving all participants on the final day of the course, allowing wider discussion on the benefits and challenges of the integrated analysis of multi-omics data.<br/>
          <b>Registration closes 22 November</b>. £975 (academia). 30 places with open registration, then selection.
        date: 8-12 March 2027
        location: EBI Hinxton, Cambridge
