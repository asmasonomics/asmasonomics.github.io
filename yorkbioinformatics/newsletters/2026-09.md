    image: /yorkbioinformatics/assets/logos/NBUG.png
    description: |
      <a href="https://northernbug.github.io/">NorthernBUG</a> brings together researchers, students and trainers 2-3 times per year across the north of England for free 1-day meetings. The focus is being a "user group" rather than only presenting polished work for a conference. <br/>
