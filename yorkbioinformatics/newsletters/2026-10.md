intro: |
  Hello everyone,<br/>
  First to highlight that Professor Mark Blaxter (Sanger Institute), director of the Darwin Tree of Life project will be delivering the bioinformatics meeting seminar <b>tomorrow (Wednesday) in B/K/018 at 1pm</b>. Mark is a great speaker and will deliver a talk over zoom about his career, the broad sequencing efforts going on across the UK and engaging with citizen science.<br/>
  We've highlighted some of the training and funding opportunities we know about below. Increasingly we're getting an AI dominance in the focus of these events. I've certainly tried this year to engage more with these developments, in terms of "actual" research (with a bioinformatics hat on), but also research support and impact on teaching. Lots going on here at the department and university level, and it's always good to hear your views as we develop a departmental strategy within the wider University messaging. The bioinformatics meeting last month led by Seth Davis on vibe coding certainly sparked lots of discussion (thanks again, Seth) and the field is obviously changing very rapidly. The department has an AI task force related to teaching and assessment (led by Tim Doheny-Adams) but we'd also like to hear more about uses in research (the good, bad and definitely the ugly...). Any feedback, <a href="https://mail.google.com/mail/?view=cm&to=andrew.mason@york.ac.uk&su=Data@York%20Newsletter%20comments%20and%20feedback" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">please get in touch</a>. There will be more to come from us over the next few months, so it would be great to have some positive use cases.<br/>
  Quick reminder to all those that use Viking - <b>the big data wipe of scratch is coming!</b> - see previous emails from IT Services. <b>Back up your data now!</b> You are almost out of time!<br/>
  If you have content for the newsletter or any feedback, <a href="https://mail.google.com/mail/?view=cm&to=andrew.mason@york.ac.uk&su=Data@York%20Newsletter%20comments%20and%20feedback" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">please get in touch</a>.<br/>
